About

I am an AI engineer, a pharmacist and a researcher based in Abuja, Nigeria. I work on applying artificial intelligence to drug discovery — virtual screening and de novo design — and I build and write about the tools that make that possible.

I am also building Magami Open Sciences Initiative, a non-profit supporting African researchers in drug discovery and open science, and I co-organise the Data Science for Health workshop at Deep Learning Indaba.
