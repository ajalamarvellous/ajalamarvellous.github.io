About

I am an AI engineer, a pharmacist and a researcher based in Abuja, Nigeria.

I currently work as an AI Engineer at SeamHealth Innovations, a healthtech consulting company, where I am responsible for architecting and building resilient, scalable AI applications. Previously I worked at Juicelife (formerly Meuve Health), an AI startup using AI and computer vision to remotely track, evaluate and quantify workouts; at Recop.it, a social commerce platform; and at Dryad Network (in collaboration with Omdena), a climatetech startup using AI and sensors to track wildfires in the Amazon. I have also worked with Lifebox Labs, a lifesciences startup building digital therapeutics for hypertensive and diabetic patients in Lagos.

I hold a Bachelor of Pharmacy from the University of Lagos, and have worked as a pharmacist at the University of Ilorin Teaching Hospital and Bowen University Teaching Hospital.

Research

My primary research interest is the application of artificial intelligence to drug discovery, specifically virtual screening and drug design. My current work is on rigorous evaluation of LLMs and generative models for drug discovery, and on genome-scale virtual screening — asking not only whether a model performs well, but understanding what the models conclusions are based on (mechanistically) and whether its conclusions are ones we should act on.

I am also building Magami Open Sciences Initiative, a non-profit open science organisation focused on collaborating with African researchers on drug discovery projects, capacity building, and the development of open-source AI tools. If you are interested in starting a collaboration, or just want to chat about what we do, email me at marvellous@magamios.org.

Beyond that, I am involved in efforts generally geared towards global health, public health and the application of AI in healthcare. I co-organise the Data Science for Health workshop at Deep Learning Indaba — 2024 in Dakar, Senegal; 2025 in Kigali, Rwanda; and 2026 in Lagos, Nigeria.

Writing

In my spare time you will catch me writing. I publish essays on Medium, Hashnode and Substack, and I keep a mailing list for disseminating them. I also run a photography page on Instagram.
